# ☕ Coffee Sales & Customer Purchasing Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Executive Summary

This project analyzes **1,000 coffee shop transactions** across multiple years to understand sales performance, product demand, customer purchasing behavior, and geographic revenue concentration.

The analysis focuses on identifying the products, markets, and customer groups contributing most to revenue and translating those findings into practical business recommendations.

### Key Business Findings

* **United States is the primary revenue market**, generating approximately **$35.6K**, or **79% of total recorded sales**.
* **Excelsa generated the highest sales among the four coffee types**, contributing approximately **$12.3K** in recorded revenue.
* **The 2.5 size category generated the highest sales**, with approximately **$23.8K**, representing more than half of total recorded revenue.
* **2021 was the strongest complete year**, generating approximately **$13.8K** in sales.
* **Customer-level analysis identifies high-value customers**, providing opportunities for targeted retention and loyalty initiatives.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer four key business questions:

1. **Which products and package sizes generate the most revenue?**
2. **Which markets contribute most to overall sales?**
3. **Which customers represent the highest recorded sales value?**
4. **How does purchasing behavior differ between loyalty-card and non-loyalty customers?**

The objective is to turn transaction-level data into insights that can support **product planning, customer retention, inventory decisions, and market prioritization**.

## 💡 Strategic Business Recommendations

### 1. Prioritize the U.S. Market

With approximately **79% of recorded sales originating from the United States**, the U.S. market should remain a primary focus for sales and inventory planning.

**Recommended action:** Monitor U.S. demand closely and ensure high-demand products are adequately stocked before major purchasing periods.

---

### 2. Focus on High-Performing Product Sizes

The **2.5 size category generated the highest recorded sales**, making it an important contributor to overall revenue.

**Recommended action:** Maintain strong availability of high-performing sizes while testing bundles or promotions to encourage customers to purchase complementary products.

---

### 3. Use Customer-Level Analysis for Retention

The analysis identifies customers generating the highest recorded sales, creating an opportunity to focus retention efforts on valuable customer segments.

**Recommended action:** Develop targeted loyalty offers or personalized promotions for high-value customers rather than applying the same strategy across the entire customer base.

---

### 4. Investigate Loyalty Program Performance

Non-loyalty customers generated more total recorded sales than loyalty-card customers in this dataset.

However, total sales alone do not establish whether the loyalty program is effective or ineffective.

**Recommended action:** Track additional measures such as **purchase frequency, average order value, repeat purchase rate, and customer lifetime value** to evaluate loyalty-program performance more accurately.

---

### 5. Use Complete-Year Comparisons for Performance Tracking

2021 was the strongest complete year in the dataset, while 2022 contains data only through **August 19**.

**Recommended action:** Use full-year comparisons when evaluating annual performance and avoid interpreting the partial 2022 total as a decline in annual sales.
