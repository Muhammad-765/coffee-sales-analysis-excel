# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis & Interactive Excel Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes coffee shop sales data using Microsoft Excel to uncover sales trends, customer purchasing patterns, product performance, and geographic sales distribution.

The project combines data preparation, lookup-based data integration, PivotTables, data visualization, and dashboard development to transform raw transactional data into an interactive business analytics solution.

The final deliverable is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to help stakeholders quickly understand sales performance and identify key business trends.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective was to analyze coffee sales data and answer key business questions such as:

* How are sales distributed across different countries?
* Which coffee types generate the highest sales?
* Which roast types perform best?
* How do sales change over time?
* Who are the highest-value customers?
* Which areas of the business contribute most to overall sales?

---

## 📈 Key Analysis

### Sales Performance

Total recorded sales in the analyzed dataset were approximately **$45.13K**, generated from **3,551 units** across **957 orders**.

### Geographic Performance

The United States generated the largest share of sales at approximately **$35.64K**, followed by:

| Country        |   Sales |
| -------------- | ------: |
| United States  | $35.64K |
| Ireland        |  $6.70K |
| United Kingdom |  $2.80K |

### Coffee Type Performance

Sales were distributed across four coffee types:

| Coffee Type |   Sales |
| ----------- | ------: |
| Excelsa     | $12.31K |
| Liberica    | $12.05K |
| Arabica     | $11.77K |
| Robusta     |  $9.01K |

Excelsa generated the highest sales among the four coffee types in this dataset.

### Roast Type Performance

| Roast Type |   Sales |
| ---------- | ------: |
| Large      | $17.35K |
| Medium     | $14.60K |
| Dark       | $13.18K |

Large-size products generated the highest sales among the size categories represented in the analysis.

### Top Customers

The analysis also identifies the highest-value customers based on total sales, enabling a closer look at customer contribution and purchasing behavior.

The top customer generated approximately **$317.07** in recorded sales.

## 💡 Key Insights

1. **The United States is the dominant sales market**, contributing substantially more revenue than Ireland and the United Kingdom.

2. **Excelsa recorded the highest sales among the coffee types**, closely followed by Liberica and Arabica.

3. **Large-size products generated the highest sales among the analyzed size categories.**

4. **Sales are distributed across multiple coffee types**, indicating that revenue is not dependent on a single product category.

5. **Customer-level analysis highlights high-value customers**, providing a foundation for customer retention and loyalty strategies.
